Audio Delay Privacy Policy

Audio Delay is a companion application designed to control a compatible external audio delay device. The app does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

Data Usage

The app uses audio delay settings and device connection information solely to provide its intended functionality. The app does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or external data storage.

Bluetooth Connectivity

Audio Delay uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to discover, connect to, and communicate with a compatible audio delay device. Bluetooth communication is used to send delay settings and receive device status and confirmation information.

This communication takes place locally between your iPhone and the connected audio delay device. No internet connection is required for normal operation.

Audio and Microphone Access

The app does not access your microphone, record audio, or process television audio. All audio processing is performed by the external audio delay device.

Data Sharing

The app does not share personal information with third parties. No user account or registration is required.
