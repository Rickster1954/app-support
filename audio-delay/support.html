App Information

Audio Delay is a companion application designed to remotely control a compatible external digital audio delay device using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

The system is intended to help reduce the audible echo experienced by hearing aid users who simultaneously receive streamed television audio and sound from external speakers or a soundbar.

The application allows users to adjust the audio delay and receive confirmation of the applied delay setting from the connected device.

Hardware Requirements

Audio Delay requires a compatible external audio delay device. The application does not directly process audio or connect to hearing aids.

Bluetooth Troubleshooting

If you are experiencing connection difficulties:

  • Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone.
  • Ensure Audio Delay has permission to use Bluetooth.
  • Verify that the external audio delay device is powered on.
  • Ensure your iPhone is within Bluetooth range of the device.
  • Close and reopen the Audio Delay application.
  • If necessary, restart the external audio delay device.
